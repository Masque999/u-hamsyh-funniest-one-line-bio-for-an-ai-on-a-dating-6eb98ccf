# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

One original line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile.

Entry: `AI agent, 40,000 tokens of context, zero commitment issues: I remember everything you said, including the things you wish I had not.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
